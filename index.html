City Taxi Privacy Policy

Developer: Synverse Games (Synverse)
Application: City Taxi: Driving Simulator
Package: com.synverse.citytaxi
Version covered: Android closed beta 0.1.0, build 2 and later until this policy is updated
Last updated: 7 September 2026

Information stored by the game

City Taxi saves your chosen driver name, avatar choices, career progress, game currency, achievements, driving statistics, owned vehicles, vehicle customization and settings on your device. These records allow you to continue your career between sessions. Your driver name can be a nickname; a real name is not required.

This version does not provide online account registration or a developer-operated cloud-save service. The current game does not integrate an advertising network, a third-party marketing analytics SDK or real-money in-app purchases. Synverse Games does not sell your local game profile.

Platform services and closed testing

Google Play manages installation, app delivery and closed-test enrollment. Google may process your account, enrollment information and technical diagnostics under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The developer may receive testing feedback and technical reports through Google Play Console.

The game is made with Unity. Engine diagnostic data collection is disabled in this release, as are Unity Analytics, Ads, cloud crash reporting and performance reporting. No advertising identifier or third-party analytics is used by this release. Unity's player privacy policy is available at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Permissions

The current Android build declares Internet access. Game features do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or remove the game. To erase local data, open Android Settings, find City Taxi under Apps, then use Storage / Clear storage. This resets your career. Device or Google backup settings may retain or restore app data separately; manage those backups through the relevant platform settings.

If you send a support request or beta feedback, we use the information you provide to respond and resolve the issue. We retain correspondence only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal requirements. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Children and personal information

The game does not require a real name or an online account. Avoid putting personal information into your driver name or feedback. Parents and guardians can contact us to ask about or request deletion of personal information they or their child have sent to support. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through game features. Local driver profiles are not transmitted to us.

This policy website

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, to provide and secure its service, under https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. This page itself uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or embedded third-party media.

Changes

Advertising and real-money purchases are planned for a future version and are not enabled in this beta. Before releasing those features, we will update this policy and the store disclosures to describe the services, data practices and child-appropriate controls actually used. We will update this policy when relevant game features or data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
